SECOND PROGRAM
lbptek.py reads the cleaned .rds files with pyreadr and shares no code with the R scripts. It rebuilds the four long
switching datasets (Study 1 and 2; Republicans facing pro-Democratic scenarios, Democrats facing pro-Republican ones),
fits Lin's estimator with CR2 cluster-robust standard errors by respondent (as estimatr::lm_lin does), and pools the
four effects and the four status-quo rates by REML random-effects meta-analysis (metafor's default). It also fits the
per-scenario models of imagined party ID (OLS with covariate dummies, HC2) and pools them, and the single-ad ATEs.
