RESULT
The package runs and all three tables reproduce exactly. bap_replication_ycp.R ran unchanged on R 4.4.3 (fixest) in
1079 s. A second program written separately in Python rebuilds the firm and sector panels from the CSV and matches
all 310 values we compared: the 100 entries of Table 1 (both panels), and in Tables 2 and 3 every coefficient,
clustered standard error and star of the three fractional-logit models, with the Ns (18,915 and 2,263) and cluster
counts (1,648 and 201).

Files used (sha256, first 16 hex digits): bap_replication_data_ycp.csv de5385ed66892cd2

SECOND PROGRAM
orzict.py reads the CSV with pandas and shares no code with the R script. It applies the documented data steps (the
four NACE sectors and thirteen firms dropped, anti-globalization = 1 - attitude, sector means by country x NACE x
year), fits the fractional logit with country fixed effects by Newton steps, and computes standard errors clustered by
firm or by country-sector. One detail matters for the third decimal: the small-sample factor counts every country
effect in K (n-1)/(n-K). Treating the country effects as nested in the clusters (not counted) makes 26 printed SEs
differ by one in the last digit.

NOT CHECKED
